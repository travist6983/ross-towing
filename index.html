For property owners

Private Property Impounds (PPI)

Unauthorized or abandoned vehicles on your property? We post a Ross Towing sign and remove unauthorized vehicles at the vehicle owner's expense, 24 hours a day.

Under Michigan Law (MCL 257.252a (Michigan Legislature, opens in a new tab)), an abandoned vehicle is any motor vehicle left on public or private property without the consent of the owner.

Want a Ross Towing sign on your property?

  1. Download and print the property owner authorization form.
  2. Fill it out and sign it.
